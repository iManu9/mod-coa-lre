# Changelog

## 2026-10-03 - First release
- Eleven Legendary runes: Hallowed Path, Eternal Conduit, Righteous Crusading Storm, Thunderous Gale, Purifying Fires of the Silver Hand, Arcing Shocks, Spreading Shocks, Crashing Storm, Walking Decay, Mass Grip, Searing Swarm.
- Two Epic runes (full-rank talents): Maelstrom Weapon, Threat of Thassarian.
- Rune items that teach their spells; ranks follow the character's level (rank 1 minimum, top rank from 60) with level-based damage scaling.
- Runebreaker's Chisel and Runekeeper Vaelith for unlearning; Runekeeper Vaelith also sells runes.
- Adaptive Training Dummy that matches its attacker's level.
- Item icons through CoA's SMSG_PATCH_ITEM packet.
- Fixes: Lightning Shield no longer procs from the wearer's own attacks (for everyone); Hurricane damage dealt by the rune; rune-fired spells leave no cooldown; area runes hit neutral targets; Searing Swarm chain reaction stopped (rune copies never re-trigger runes).
